Farm Capitalist: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Farm Capitalist ("the game") is an idle farming game for Android made by Causality Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game and the services it uses handle, why, and what choices you have. Contact: dev.causality@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your farm is saved on your own device and is not sent to us.
  • The game shows ads and sells optional in-app purchases. The services that do this (Google AdMob, Google Play and RevenueCat) process some technical data, described below.
  • With your agreement where the law requires it, the game sends anonymous game statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase) so we can fix problems and balance the game. You can switch this off in Settings.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (farm, gold, gems, settings and purchase status) is saved in encrypted storage on your device. We do not receive it, and it is not backed up to a cloud service by the game. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Information handled by the services in the game

ServiceWhat it is used forData it may process
Google AdMob (Google) Showing rewarded video ads, which you can choose to watch for in-game rewards. Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice first.
Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google) Understanding how the game is used (for example, which crops players unlock) and finding and fixing crashes. Game events (such as crop unlocked, mission claimed, purchase made, ad rewarded), an app instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, approximate region derived from the IP address, and crash logs and diagnostics. Events never contain your name, email address or other directly identifying details.
RevenueCat Managing in-app purchases and subscriptions (gem packs, Starter Pack, VIP Farm Club) and restoring them. A randomly generated anonymous user ID, purchase and subscription records, and device and app information. Your payment details are handled by Google Play and are never seen by us or RevenueCat.
Google Play (Google) Distributing the game and processing payments. Handled under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

These providers act under their own privacy policies: Google, Firebase and RevenueCat. Some of them process data in countries outside your own, including the United States, under safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

3. Your choices

  • Anonymous stats. In the game, open Settings and switch Anonymous stats off to stop game statistics and crash reports. If you are in a region where consent is required, nothing is collected until you agree in the consent message.
  • Ad consent. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, Settings shows Privacy choices, where you can change or withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Advertising ID. In your Android settings you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation (Settings > Privacy > Ads, the exact path depends on your device).
  • Purchases. Manage or cancel subscriptions in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions.

4. Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

We rely on your consent for advertising identifiers and for analytics and crash reporting, which you can withdraw at any time as described above. We rely on performance of a contract to process purchases you make, and on our legitimate interest in running and securing the game for the technical data needed to deliver it.

5. Retention

We keep no accounts and no copy of your farm. Analytics and crash data is kept by Google for a limited period set in our Firebase project settings and is then deleted automatically. Purchase records are kept by RevenueCat and Google Play as long as needed to provide the purchases and to meet legal obligations.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not know who you are, we usually cannot look up your data without help from you: tell us what you need using the contact address above, and we will help where we can. Resetting your advertising ID and uninstalling the game also detach the game from the data collected so far. If you are in the EEA, you can complain to your local data protection authority (in Sweden: Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY).

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and publish the new version at this address. If a change affects how your data is used in a significant way, we will also tell you in the game.
