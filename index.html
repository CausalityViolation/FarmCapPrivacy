Farm Capitalist: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Farm Capitalist ("the game") is an idle farming game for Android made by Causality ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game and the services it uses handle, why, and what choices you have. Contact: dev.causality@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You do not need an account to play. We do not ask for your phone number, contacts, photos or location. The only time the game asks who you are is if you choose the optional cloud save (section 4), which signs you in with your Google account.
  • Your farm is saved on your own device. Two optional features send some data online: the leaderboard (section 3), where your farm name, farmer look and Golden Seeds are stored so other players can see them, and cloud save (section 4), where a scrambled copy of your farm is kept in your Google account so you can restore it on a new phone.
  • The game shows ads and sells optional in-app purchases. The services that do this (Google AdMob, Google Play and RevenueCat) process some technical data, described below.
  • With your agreement where the law requires it, the game sends anonymous game statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase) so we can fix problems and balance the game. You can switch this off in Settings.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (farm, gold, gems, settings and purchase status) is saved in encrypted storage on your device. We do not receive it, and the game does not back it up anywhere unless you turn on cloud save (section 4). Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes the copy on your device.

3. The leaderboard (optional)

The game has a "Top Farmers" leaderboard. It is off until you tap Join the race; before that, nothing is sent and no player ID is created. When you join, the game signs in anonymously (no account, no email) and stores your farm name (two words you pick from lists; you cannot type your own), your farmer look, your Golden Seeds and the Golden Seeds you earned this week. Other players who open the leaderboard can see these items next to your place; nothing else about you is shown.

You can leave at any time with Leave leaderboard in the leaderboard screen, which deletes your entries. Choosing Reset farm in Settings deletes them too, and entries of players who have not opened the game for about 90 days are removed automatically. Weekly entries are removed a few weeks after their week ends.

4. Cloud save (optional)

Cloud save is off until you turn it on in Settings. Before that, nothing is sent and you are not signed in. When you turn it on, the game opens Google's own sign-in screen and you pick a Google account. The game then keeps one copy of your farm in that account's storage, so it can come back on a new phone or after you reinstall the game. If you play on two phones, it also keeps them in step, and asks you which farm to keep when they are different, so that one is never replaced without your choice.

What is stored. Firebase Authentication keeps your Google account ID, email address and name for the account you picked. We can see them in our Firebase project, we do not show them to other players, and they are not part of your farm. Cloud Firestore keeps your farm: your progress, gems, settings and the status of your purchases, encrypted by the game, together with the time it was saved. Only you, signed in to that account, can read or change it. The sign-in links your leaderboard player ID to your Google account, if you also use the leaderboard; other players still see only the farm name, farmer look and Golden Seeds described in section 3.

What it is used for. Only to restore and update your farm. We do not use your email address to contact you, for advertising or for analytics.

Turning it off. Settings > Cloud save > switch off. The game stops saving to the cloud on that phone. The copy that is already there stays until you delete it, so you can turn it on again later.

Deleting your cloud save and account. In the game, open Settings > Cloud save > Delete cloud data and tap twice. This deletes the cloud copy of your farm, your leaderboard entries and the Firebase account for your Google sign-in. Your farm on the phone is not touched. You can also ask us to do it by emailing the contact address below from the address of your Google account, and we will delete it within 30 days.

5. Your choices

  • Leaderboard. Joining is optional. Use Leave leaderboard to remove your entries.
  • Cloud save. Optional. Switch it off in Settings > Cloud save, or use Delete cloud data to remove the cloud copy and the account link (section 4).
  • Anonymous stats. In the game, open Settings and switch Anonymous stats off to stop game statistics and crash reports. If you are in a region where consent is required, nothing is collected until you agree in the consent message.
  • Ad consent. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, Settings shows Privacy choices, where you can change or withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Advertising ID. In your Android settings you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation (Settings > Privacy > Ads, the exact path depends on your device).
  • Purchases. Manage or cancel subscriptions in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions.

6. Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

We rely on your consent for advertising identifiers and for analytics and crash reporting, which you can withdraw at any time as described above. We rely on performance of a contract to process purchases you make and to run the leaderboard and the cloud save you choose to use, and on our legitimate interest in running and securing the game for the technical data needed to deliver it.

7. Retention

We keep no copy of your farm unless you turn on cloud save. The cloud copy, and the Google account ID, email address and name that Firebase Authentication keeps for it, are kept until you delete them (section 4) or ask us to. Leaderboard entries are kept until you leave the leaderboard or reset your farm, and are deleted automatically after about 90 days without the game being opened (weekly entries a few weeks after their week ends). Analytics and crash data is kept by Google for a limited period set in our Firebase project settings and is then deleted automatically. Purchase records are kept by RevenueCat and Google Play as long as needed to provide the purchases and to meet legal obligations.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not know who you are (unless you used cloud save, where the email address of your Google account lets us find it), we usually cannot look up your data without help from you: tell us what you need using the contact address above, and we will help where we can. Resetting your advertising ID and uninstalling the game also detach the game from the data collected so far. If you are in the EEA, you can complain to your local data protection authority (in Sweden: Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY).

9. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and publish the new version at this address. If a change affects how your data is used in a significant way, we will also tell you in the game.
